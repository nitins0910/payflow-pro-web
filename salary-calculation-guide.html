How Net Pay Is Calculated

The exact formulas, statutory rules, and rounding conventions PayFlow Pro applies to every row on the Salary Calculation page.

This page documents the actual calculation logic built into the app — not a generic payroll explainer. Every rate, ceiling, and rounding rule below is editable in Salary Calculation → Settings (PF rate/ceiling, ESI rate/limit, PT state), because statutory values are revised from time to time and company policies vary. Where a figure is described as a "default", please verify it against the current government notification for your state before relying on it for compliance.

1 Gross Salary & LOP Proration

Every earning component — Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, and Other Allowances — is entered as a full-month figure and then prorated for that employee's LOP (Loss of Pay) days for the payroll month, using whichever Days-in-Month Method you've selected (Fixed 30-Day Month, or Actual Calendar Days).

Proration Factor
Factor = (Days in Month − LOP Days) / Days in Month
Prorated Gross
Prorated Gross = (Basic + HRA + Special Allowance + Other Allowances) × Factor
🛡️ Input safety
Negative inputs — a negative salary component, or LOP days greater than the days in the month — are automatically floored to 0 before the formula runs, so a data-entry mistake can never produce a negative proration factor or a negative earning line.
Worked example — 30-day month, 5 LOP days
Days in Month30
LOP Days5
Factor(30 − 5) / 30 = 0.8333
Full-month Gross (Basic+HRA+Special+Other)₹30,000
Prorated Gross₹25,000.00

2 Provident Fund (EPF)

⚖️ Employees' Provident Funds & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952

PF is deducted from the employee at a default rate of 12%, applied to Basic pay only — not the full gross — and is subject to the statutory wage ceiling of ₹15,000.

⚠️ The ceiling is prorated too
A detail that's easy to get wrong: PayFlow Pro doesn't apply the flat ₹15,000 ceiling as-is when there's LOP. The ceiling itself is scaled by the same proration factor as Basic pay — so an employee who worked exactly half the month has their PF ceiling reduced to ₹7,500 for that month, not compared against the full ₹15,000.
Effective PF Wage
Prorated Ceiling = PF Ceiling (₹15,000) × Factor PF Wage = Min( Prorated Basic , Prorated Ceiling )
PF Deduction
PF = Round( PF Wage × PF Rate )
✅ EPFO rounding rule
Per EPFO convention, the final PF deduction is mathematically rounded to the nearest rupee using Math.round() — ₹1,240.40 becomes ₹1,240, and ₹1,240.50 becomes ₹1,241. This is different from ESI's rounding rule below — don't mix the two up.
Worked example — same employee, Basic ₹18,000/month
Prorated Basic (0.8333 × ₹18,000)₹15,000.00
Prorated Ceiling (0.8333 × ₹15,000)₹12,500.00
PF Wage = Min(15,000, 12,500)₹12,500.00
12,500 × 12%₹1,500.00
PF Deduction (rounded)₹1,500

3 Employee's State Insurance (ESI)

⚖️ Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948

ESI is deducted at a default employee rate of 0.75%, and only applies to employees within the statutory gross-salary coverage limit — ₹21,000 per month (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities).

⚠️ Eligibility check vs. deduction base are different figures
This is the single most misunderstood part of ESI, and PayFlow Pro handles it deliberately:
  • Eligibility is checked against the employee's full, unprorated monthly gross (i.e. as if there were zero LOP days). If that full gross is ≤ ₹21,000, the employee is ESI-applicable for the entire contribution period — LOP in a given month does not remove them from coverage.
  • The actual rupee deduction, however, is calculated on the employee's prorated, paid gross for that specific month — so someone on LOP still gets ESI charged only on what they actually earned that month.
Eligibility (checked once, on full gross)
ESI Applicable = Full Monthly Gross ≤ ESI Limit (₹21,000)
ESI Deduction (if applicable)
ESI = Ceil( Prorated Paid Gross × ESI Rate )
✅ ESI Rule 51 — round UP, always
Per ESI (General) Regulation, Rule 51, effective 1 October 2004, the ESI contribution is never rounded to the nearest rupee — it is always rounded up to the next higher rupee using Math.ceil(), even when the decimal portion is as small as ₹0.01. A computed value of ₹187.01 is deducted as ₹188, not ₹187.
Worked example — Full Gross ₹20,000/month, 5 LOP days this month
Full Monthly Gross₹20,000 (≤ ₹21,000 → eligible)
Prorated Paid Gross this month₹16,666.67
16,666.67 × 0.75%₹125.0000
ESI Deduction (ceil)₹125

4 Professional Tax (PT)

⚖️ Article 276, Constitution of India + State Enactments

Professional Tax is a state-level tax, not a central one — every state sets its own slabs, and several states (e.g. Karnataka, Maharashtra, West Bengal, Telangana/Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha) have different monetary thresholds and monthly amounts. PayFlow Pro applies an indicative default PT slab based on the Gross Salary for that month and the state you select under Salary Calculation → State (Professional Tax).

  • PT is computed automatically per row when you set the state and click "Re-apply PT to all rows".
  • Because slab amounts change periodically by state notification, the PT figure is only a starting point — you can manually override the PT amount on any individual employee row at any time, and your edit is preserved rather than being silently recalculated.

5 TDS & Other Deductions

⚖️ Income Tax Act, 1961

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) under the Income Tax Act, and any "Other Deductions" line (e.g. loan recoveries, advances), are always entered manually per employee — PayFlow Pro does not compute income-tax slabs, exemptions, or Section 80C-style declarations on your behalf. As with every other numeric input on this page, negative values are not permitted and are floored to 0.

6 Final Net Pay

Net Pay
Net Pay = Prorated Gross − ( PF + ESI + PT + TDS + Other Deductions )
🛡️ Floor at zero
If total deductions ever exceed the Prorated Gross for a row (e.g. very high LOP combined with a large manual TDS/Other entry), Net Pay is floored at ₹0 — it is never shown or exported as a negative amount.
Putting it together — the running example above
Prorated Gross₹25,000.00
Less: PF− ₹1,500
Less: ESI− ₹0 (gross exceeds ₹21,000, not applicable here)
Less: PT (Karnataka slab)− ₹200
Less: TDS− ₹0
Less: Other Deductions− ₹0
Net Pay₹23,300.00
