Privacy Policy

Effective date: [16 August 2026]  ·  Version 1

This is a general-purpose template drafted for PayFlow Pro's actual data flows (Firebase Authentication, Firestore storage, Razorpay payments, and Gmail-based support email). It is provided for convenience and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer — please have it reviewed before relying on it, particularly for compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what data PayFlow Pro (operated by EnginX & Co) collects when you use our website and dashboard (the "Service"), why we collect it, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and the choices available to you. It applies to you as a registered user, and — where you enter their details — to the employees whose payroll data you manage through your account.

2. Data we collect

3. Where your data is stored

Account and payroll data is stored in Firebase Authentication and Firestore (Google Cloud infrastructure), scoped so that each account's data is only readable by that account when signed in — access is enforced both by Firebase Authentication and by Firestore security rules. Fields controlling your credit balance can only ever be written by our server-side functions, never directly by any browser, including your own.

4. Who we share data with

  • Razorpay — when you purchase export credits, your payment is processed by Razorpay. We send Razorpay the amount and your account identifier to create the order, and we verify the payment result they return; we do not receive or store your underlying payment instrument details.
  • Google / Firebase — our hosting, authentication, and database provider. Google processes data on our behalf under its own data-processing terms.
  • Gmail (support inbox) — messages you send via Help & Support are emailed to our support inbox with your account email set as the reply-to address, so replies go straight back to you. We do not use a separate ticketing system.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction: you can view and edit your company and employee records directly within the app at any time.
  • Deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting nitins1009@gmail.com, subject to any records we are legally required to retain (e.g. for tax or audit purposes).
  • Consent withdrawal: you may stop using the Service at any time. Where our processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw it, though this may mean we can no longer provide the Service to you.
  • If you are located in India, these rights are provided consistent with the DPDP Act, 2023. You may also have additional rights depending on your location.

6. Employee data you enter

When you add an employee's details to run payroll, you are acting as the data controller for that information — you are responsible for having a lawful basis (such as the employment relationship) and, where required, the employee's consent to process their data through PayFlow Pro. We act as a data processor for that employee data, handling it only to provide the Service to you.

7. Data retention

We retain account, company, and payroll data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, tax, and audit obligations and to resolve disputes. Export/audit history is retained to give you a permanent record of past payroll runs unless you request deletion.

8. Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Firestore security rules to restrict data access to your own account, and server-side (Netlify Functions) enforcement for anything that affects billing or payments — none of which can be bypassed from the browser. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

9. Children's privacy

PayFlow Pro is a business tool not directed at children, and is not knowingly used by anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be flagged with a new version number, and you will be asked to review and accept the updated policy the next time you sign in before continuing to use the Service.

11. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Reach us at nitins1009@gmail.com or via the Help & Support option inside the app.
