📲 Add to Home Screen

Add PayFlow Pro to your phone's home screen and it opens full-screen, just like an installed app — no App Store download needed. Your login and wallet stay exactly the same, this just adds a shortcut.

  1. Open PayFlow Pro in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three-dot) menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").
  4. Confirm by tapping "Add" / "Install".
  5. A PayFlow Pro icon now appears on your home screen — tap it any time to open the app full-screen.

About PayFlow Pro

PayFlow Pro is a payroll management and salary-disbursement tool built for small and mid-sized businesses. It replaces spreadsheet-based payroll with a single dashboard where you can maintain your employee records, run monthly payroll, and generate the exact bank-upload files your bank needs to actually pay everyone.

What you can do here:

  • Maintain an employee directory — names, salaries, bank account numbers, and IFSC codes — either one by one or via bulk CSV import, with validation that catches duplicate or malformed account details before they're saved.
  • Run a Payroll Run for any month: pick employees, confirm amounts, and disburse — every run is recorded with a batch ID so it can be traced later.
  • Generate bank-ready export files formatted for direct bulk upload to your bank's salary/NEFT portal, instead of manually entering each transfer.
  • Keep a full Activity Log of every change made to employee records and every export generated, for audit and accountability.
  • Manage your company details (name, bank, account number, IFSC) that get stamped onto every export file.

How billing works: every new account starts with 50 free credits. Generating one payroll export file costs 5 credits. Once your balance runs out, you can top up your wallet with a credit pack — the bigger the pack, the bigger the per-credit discount. See the Wallet page for current pack pricing.

Your data: all employee records, company details, and export history are stored securely per account and are only ever accessible to you when signed in — nothing is shared across accounts.
