PayFlow Pro

// add it to your home screen

Add PayFlow Pro to your phone's home screen and it opens full-screen, just like an installed app — no App Store or Play Store download needed. Your login and wallet stay exactly the same; this just adds a shortcut. Pick your phone's platform below.

  1. Open PayFlow Pro in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three-dot) menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").
  4. Confirm by tapping "Add" / "Install".
  5. A PayFlow Pro icon now appears on your home screen — tap it any time to open the app full-screen.
  1. Open PayFlow Pro in Safari on your iPhone/iPad (this only works in Safari, not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share icon □↑ in the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm the name and tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
  5. A PayFlow Pro icon now appears on your home screen — tap it any time to open the app full-screen.
Already installed? Just open the PayFlow Pro icon from your home screen — you don't need to repeat these steps or download anything else.
